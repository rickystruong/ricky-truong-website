About


Hello! I'm Ricky, a sophomore at Harvard University studying Statistics and Mathematics.


I'm a first-generation college student from New Hampshire, but I'm happy to call the city of Cambridge my second index. On campus, I'm involved with the Harvard Vietnamese Association, Harvard Open Data Project, Group for Undergraduates in Statistics, and Harvard Running Club.


Additionally, I'm a Teaching Fellow for STAT 100 ("Introduction to Statistics and Data Science"). In this role, I've developed supplementary material, including practice problems and review slides.

Interests


Broadly, I'm interested in data science—theory as well as application. Over the summer, I worked with Dr. Sebastian Munoz-Najar Galvez on his project "Writing for College Admissions with AI Assistance" as a Research Assistant.


I'm also passionate about educational equity. I've held various roles at Breakthrough Manchester, a non-profit supporting underrepresented students on their path to college. In my most recent position, I serve as an advisor to high school students in my indextown.


Outside of academics, I enjoy spending time outdoors and with friends.
